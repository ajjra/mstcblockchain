About MST Blockchain

MST Blockchain is a public Layer 1 blockchain with EVM compatibility. It is designed to support decentralized applications, smart contracts, digital assets, and enterprise blockchain use cases.


Developers can use familiar Web3 tools such as Solidity, MetaMask, Remix, and Hardhat to build applications on the network.

Blockchain Technology

Proof of Stake Authority-based consensus architecture for network validation.

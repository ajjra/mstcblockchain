About MST Blockchain

MST Blockchain is a public Layer 1 blockchain with EVM compatibility. It is designed to support decentralized applications, smart contracts, digital assets and blockchain-based use cases.

Blockchain Technology

PoSA

Proof of Stake Authority based consensus architecture for network validation.

EVM Compatible

Supports Ethereum-compatible smart contract functionality and EVM-based applications.

Validators

Validators participate in maintaining and validating the blockchain network.

Smart Contracts

Smart contracts can support decentralized applications and blockchain-based services.

Low Fees

MST publishes a low average transaction fee figure for its network.

Web3

The network can support Web3 applications, digital assets and decentralized ecosystems.

MST Ecosystem
